Why we're doing this

City dogs live smaller lives than we like to admit.

Same lift. Same lamp post. Same twenty minutes, twice a day.

On a leash. Alone.

Most dogs in Hyderabad go weeks without properly meeting another dog. So when they finally see one across the road, they lose it — barking, pulling, straining at the leash. We call it bad behaviour. It's usually just a dog with nowhere to put any of it.

One morning a year doesn't fix that. But it is a very good morning — and your dog will sleep like he's earned it.

Before you sign up

The things people ask us.

My dog is nervous or reactive around other dogs.

There's a quieter group that starts at the back, with space around it. Tell us in the form and we'll put you there. Nobody has to be in the middle of the crowd.

Is it a race?

No. There's no timing and no positions. The fastest dog and the slowest dog get exactly the same thing: a tee, a goodie bag, and a very tired evening.

My dog is small / senior / still a puppy.

1KM is short on purpose — it's about twenty minutes at a comfortable walk. Carry them for a stretch if they've had enough. Puppies should have finished their core vaccinations.

Do I need to be fit?

It's one kilometre and you can walk all of it. If you can do your normal evening walk, you can do this.

Can I bring two dogs?

Yes, if you can comfortably handle both on leash on your own. Register once and mention it in the form so we pack a second goodie bag.

What if it rains that morning?

We move it. Your spot carries over automatically and we'll message you on WhatsApp — you don't need to do anything.

What if I can't make it after all?

Full refund up to 48 hours before the run. Just message us on WhatsApp or Instagram.

Is there parking at Necklace Road?

Yes. We'll send the exact meeting point and the closest parking on WhatsApp the day before.
